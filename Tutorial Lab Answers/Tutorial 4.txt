Hotel Information System
1. Given the context of the hotel information system as discussed in Lecture 2 and Tutorial
Week 3,
a. What are the quality requirements relevant to the Check-In and Check-Out tasks?
Robustness, Portability, Usability

b. What quality requirements are similar and what not, between these two tasks?
All of them

c. Please specify each of these requirements, in a precise and verifiable manner.
Robustness:
"System must experience less than 10 errors over a 6 month period."

Portability:
"System must be usable on an iPhone 16 and the standard hotel computer with Windows 10 installed."

Usability:
"Users must be able to understand and use the main functions of the solution after 5 minutes of play time."

2. In general, considering all working areas, what would be the top 3 to 5 quality attributes
applicable to the Hotel Information System?
Robustness, Portability, Usability


3. What are the general software quality attributes? Describe each of the software quality
attributes.
Modifiability: System's ability and ease to be added to
Availability: System's uptime
Usability: System's ease of use
Security: System's ability to protect and mitigate against attacks
Reliability: System's ability to not experience errors (measured in downtime/time spent maintaining system)


4. What are some forms of scalability? How do they work?
Scale-up: Scaling vertically
Scale-out: Scaling horizontally

5. What is Throughput? Compare and contrast average and peek throughput.
Throughput is the rate at which data is successfully transferred through a system or network, or the quantity of work a system completes in a given time. It is typically measured in TPS or transactions per second.

6. How does the quality attribute of security affect availability in terms of the authentication
and the authorization process?
If strict security measured are put in place for a system to be highly secure, it can disallow certain users from signing up who may not have the capabilities or the want to set up MFA for example - thereby decreasing availability.

7. Software qualities can be categorised into two (2) broad categories: external and internal.
Please explain both, including an example. 
External qualities are those that are affected by or can be seen visually. An example is a user interacting with an interface and getting an error message.
Internal qualities are those unseen metrics that affect the innerworkings of a solution. An example of this is the performance of a solution as measured by their throughput.
